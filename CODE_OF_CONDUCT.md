# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in the SYNTRA project a welcoming, respectful, and constructive experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, professional, and considerate.
- Discuss technical decisions using evidence and reproducible observations.
- Accept constructive feedback and focus on the problem rather than the person.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish credentials, private source code, personal information, or sensitive operational data.
- Keep criticism, disagreement, and review focused on technical merit and project impact.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, doxxing, threats, and publication of private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove content and may close discussions or restrict participation when conduct violates this policy. Reports should be sent privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Maintainers will review reports with discretion and prioritize the safety and privacy of participants.
